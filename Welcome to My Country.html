Xush kelibsiz · Uzbekistan

Don’t just see Uzbekistan. Take part in it.

Cook somsa in Jizzakh, embroider suzani in Samarkand, dance lazgi in Khorezm, and walk on stage in an adras robe on your final evening. You choose what to try. We book the masters, performers, guide and transport.

  • 10 days · 5 regions
  • 12 hands-on activities
  • 8–15 travellers per group

What you will do

Six traditions, learned with your own hands

Each session is led by a working master or performer, with an English-speaking assistant beside you.

Food · Jizzakh

Jizzakh somsa

Fold and bake the region’s famous large somsa in a clay tandoor.

Craft · Samarkand

Suzani embroidery

Learn the stitches and the meaning of the patterns from the women who sew them.

Dance · Khorezm

Lazgi

The fiery dance of Khorezm, taught step by step until you can dance it through.

Music · Tashkent

Doira and dutar

Hold the frame drum and the two-stringed lute, and play along with the musicians.

Craft · Bukhara

Miniature painting

Paint a miniature with a Bukhara master and keep your work.

Living theatre · Bukhara

A scene from the Silk Road

Actors bring a historic site to life, and you are given a role in the story.

Three ways to join in

Go as far as you feel comfortable

Every activity is offered at three depths, so a shy first-timer and a keen learner can travel in the same group.

30–60 minutes

Try

A few dance steps, a turn on the doira, a simple object made beside a craftsperson.

2–3 hours

Learn

A full workshop in a craft, or a dance learned from start to finish.

Final evening · optional

Perform

Your dance or a role in a historical scene, in national dress.

How it works

One booking for the whole programme

  1. Tell us what you want to try

    Pick the activities and how deep you want to go.

  2. We arrange everyone

    Masters, performers, a guide and transport are booked for you.

  3. Arrive and take part

    A local artist works beside you in every session.
